Incident: Payment Service Elevated 500 Errors

Date: 2026-08-15

10:02 UTC - Monitoring detected an increase in HTTP 500 responses from the payment-service.

10:04 UTC - On-call engineer acknowledged the alert.

10:07 UTC - Application logs showed repeated database connection timeout errors.

10:10 UTC - Database monitoring showed the application connection pool operating near its configured maximum.

10:14 UTC - Traffic to the payment-service was temporarily reduced.

10:17 UTC - Error rates began to decline.

10:22 UTC - Payment success rates returned to normal.

Customer impact:
Some customers were unable to complete payment transactions between approximately 10:02 UTC and 10:22 UTC.

Observed evidence:
- HTTP 500 error rate increased sharply.
- Database connection timeout errors were present in application logs.
- Connection pool utilization was near its configured maximum.
- Error rates decreased after traffic was reduced.

Investigation notes:
The available evidence suggests database connection pool exhaustion may have contributed to the failures. The investigation has not yet conclusively established this as the confirmed root cause.

Response observations:
- Monitoring detected the incident quickly.
- The on-call engineer acknowledged the alert within two minutes.
- Engineers had to manually correlate application logs and database metrics.
- No dedicated alert existed for connection pool saturation.
